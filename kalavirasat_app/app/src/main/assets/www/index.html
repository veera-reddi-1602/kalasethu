About This Craft

Kondapalli Toys – The Colourful Wooden Heritage of Andhra Pradesh

Discover the timeless beauty of Kondapalli Toys, a traditional wooden craft originating from Kondapalli, near Vijayawada in Andhra Pradesh. Handcrafted from lightweight Tella Poniki wood, these colourful creations portray village life, farmers, animals, musicians, mythological characters, and traditional celebrations.

Created through generations of inherited craftsmanship, every toy reflects the creativity, cultural knowledge, and artistic identity of its makers. Recognised with a Geographical Indication (GI) tag in 2007, Kondapalli Toys remain an important part of Andhra Pradesh's handicraft heritage.

Explore their history, discover the traditional carving and painting techniques, and learn how artisan communities preserve this remarkable art. Through Kalavirasat's digital heritage experience, connect with the stories, traditions, and craftsmanship behind every creation.

🏛️ History & Cultural Significance
⌄

The art of Kondapalli toy making boasts a 400-year legacy tracing back to the 16th century, patronized by the rulers of the Reddy and Vijayanagara dynasties. Carried forward by the Aryakshatriya artisan community who migrated from Rajasthan, these sacred dolls are historically gifted during Navaratri and Sankranti Bommala Koluvu celebrations.

⚙️ Materials & Making Process
⌄

Crafted exclusively from Tella Poniki (Givotia rottleriformis), a soft, pliable, and sustainable medicinal wood. After hand-carving individual limbs and features, artisans join the parts using Lappam (a natural paste of sawdust and tamarind seed glue). Once smooth, artisans apply vibrant non-toxic lacquers, water-based enamel, and natural vegetable dyes.
